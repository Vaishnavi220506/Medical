## What a meningioma is
A meningioma grows from the meninges, the membranes that cover the brain and spinal cord. It is the most common primary brain tumor in adults and is more common in women. About 80% are WHO grade 1 (benign) and grow slowly; grade 2 (atypical) and grade 3 (anaplastic) meningiomas are less common and more likely to recur.

## Meningioma on MRI
Meningiomas are usually well-circumscribed, extra-axial masses (outside the brain tissue itself) attached to the dura, with strong, uniform enhancement after contrast. A "dural tail" (enhancing thickening of the adjacent dura) is a characteristic sign. They can cause edema in the adjacent brain and sometimes thickening of nearby bone.

## Management
Small meningiomas without symptoms are often monitored with periodic MRI. Symptomatic or growing tumors may be treated with surgery, stereotactic radiosurgery or radiation therapy.
